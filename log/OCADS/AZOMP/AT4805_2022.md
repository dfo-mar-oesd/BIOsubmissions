## Lessons Learned

1. **Always review auto-generated metadata** - Reid's scripts may not produce appropriate titles/abstracts
2. **Manual metadata entry required** for SDIS platform with current templates
3. **Lat/lon warnings are expected** when using bottle-level coordinates
4. **Date formats must be verified** from automated scripts
5. **Check analyzing instruments** for nutrients - filter types are not instruments
6. **Verify sampling methods** - CTD vs Niskin bottle assignments
